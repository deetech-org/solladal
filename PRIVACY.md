# Privacy Policy

**App:** சொல்லாடல் (Solladal) — Tamil Word Game  
**Owner:** deetech.org  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-22  

---

## Summary

**சொல்லாடல் (Solladal) collects nothing.** It has no accounts, no user logins, no backend servers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and makes zero network requests. The game runs 100% locally and offline on your device, evaluating guesses and loading clues directly from its built-in offline word repository. Nothing you type or do in the app is ever transmitted, tracked, or shared.

---

## What the App Stores

- **Game Statistics**: Your win streaks, games played count, win percentages, and guess distribution histograms are stored **locally on your device** (via the browser/OS secure local sandbox storage) so your progress is remembered across game sessions.
- **Current Game State**: The active puzzle state and letter placements are stored **locally on your device** to prevent losing your progress if you switch apps.

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any external server.

---

## What the App Does NOT Do

- **No personal data collection**: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location data.
- **No device identifiers**: No advertising IDs, IDFA, Android Advertising ID, or fingerprinting.
- **No tracking or analytics**: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or third-party telemetry.
- **No advertising**: Zero banner ads, interstitial ads, or tracking cookies.
- **No accounts or passwords**: No login or registration required.

---

## Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. The app is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines, and the Google Play Families Policy.

---

## Your Control Over Your Data

All game data is local to your device. You can reset your statistics at any time from the in-app settings/stats menu, and uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally saved data.
